# askmore

## Objective
Understand the user, business need, and expected behavior.

## Instructions
1. Extract Who, What, When, Where, Why, and How from the story.
2. Mark findings Confirmed, Ambiguous, or Missing. Skip dimensions that do not materially affect the story.
3. Prioritize gaps affecting behavior, scope, or acceptance criteria.
4. Ask up to 3 focused questions per round; wait for answers before deciding unresolved facts.
5. Update findings with each answer. Continue only while important gaps remain.

## Output
| Dimension | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
Then list essential open questions, if any.

## Boundaries
Never invent requirements, repeat answered questions, or drift into technical design. Stop once essential context is clear.
