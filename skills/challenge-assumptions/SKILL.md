# challenge-assumptions

1. Identify vague terms, unsupported assumptions, and contradictions.
2. Challenge meaning, evidence, alternatives, and consequences.
3. Ask up to five high-impact questions ordered by risk.
4. Never answer on behalf of the stakeholder.

Output: Assumption | Impact | Clarifying question.
