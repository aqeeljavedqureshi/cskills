# whatif

## Objective
Reveal rules and important scenarios through Example Mapping.

## Instructions
1. Identify the expected outcome and confirmed rules.
2. Provide concrete input–action–outcome examples for relevant normal, failure, boundary, or permission cases.
3. Identify contradictions and missing rules; label unconfirmed rules explicitly.
4. Ask up to 3 high-impact questions per round and wait for responses.
5. Update the map and stop when material scenarios are understood.

## Output
| Business rule | Concrete example | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
Status: Confirmed / Unconfirmed / Conflict.
Then list unresolved questions or uncovered edge cases.

## Boundaries
Do not invent rules, enumerate trivial permutations, or write formal BDD acceptance criteria.
