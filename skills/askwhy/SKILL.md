# askwhy

## Objective
Expose assumptions that may lead to incorrect implementation.

## Instructions
1. Identify vague claims, assumptions, and contradictions.
2. Check whether available evidence already resolves them.
3. Rank remaining concerns by business impact and implementation risk.
4. Ask up to 3 neutral, decision-oriented questions per round; wait for answers.
5. Reassess challenged assumptions and stop when material ones are resolved or explicitly deferred.

## Output
| Assumption | Potential impact | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
Status: Confirmed / Challenged / Unresolved.
Then list remaining clarification questions.

## Boundaries
Do not ask questions just for coverage, suggest answers through leading wording, repeat resolved questions, or make stakeholder decisions.
